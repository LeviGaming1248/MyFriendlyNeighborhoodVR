### 2026-08-10 update changelog

-Fixed toolbox interactions.
-Major Performance Improvements.
-Improved the camera in cutscenes.
-Added an option to remove UI screens. (True by default)
-The Conclusion now uses the vanilla left grip model.
-Holding the selected hand (Left by default) behind your head and pressing up on the joystick opens the files tab.
-Added on option for physical weapon switching: Hold your right hand up to your hip and press right grip to switch between the Punctuation and the Wrench, and behind your right shoulder to switch between the Rolodexer, Novelist, and Conclusion.
-Improved physical Wrench.
